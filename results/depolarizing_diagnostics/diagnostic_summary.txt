Interpretation guide
--------------------
1. Small fit residuals for both ensembles support the claim that the measured output channel is approximately a global mixture with uniform noise, even when the circuit is structured.
2. If structured residuals are large but the entropy prediction still covers the TVD, the apparent success is not explained by the global-depolarizing model and should be treated as empirical.
3. If alpha_fit tracks QCAP error, CB/QCAP is estimating the same effective white-noise strength used by the entropy correction.
4. If alpha_fit is systematically below QCAP, the baseline remains conservative and the entropy correction may still work as an upper prediction. If alpha_fit exceeds QCAP, violations are expected.
5. Repeat with different noise mechanisms separately: stochastic Pauli only, coherent only, spectator/correlated, and drift. This identifies which channels preserve or destroy the white-noise fit.
